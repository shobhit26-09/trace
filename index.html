What Trace can check

GitHub, Bluesky, GitLab and DEV are checked through unauthenticated public API endpoints. Profiles can show a public name, bio, follower counts, declared location, joined date and self-linked websites when the source supplies them. No private contact data is collected.

X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube links are manual candidates only, not scraped or claimed as found. Custom Bluesky domains are supported; bare handles use .bsky.social. Searches are limited to ten per five minutes per IP. Platform quotas may limit coverage.

The backend holds up to 200 short-lived reports in memory. It does not save profiles to a database. Hosting providers may retain standard request logs. Use for your own digital footprint or consent-based research, never harassment.
